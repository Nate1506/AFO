# Say It Again: 2-minute pitch clip

Goal: show the problem, the core loop, and why it is fun and doable, in 120 seconds.
Judging: problem (20), fun and design (30), feasibility and impact (30), presentation (20).

## Before recording

- Tool: OBS Studio, 1920x1080, 60 fps. Capture the game window plus your microphone; add game audio as a separate track.
- Game: Settings → Language ภาษาไทย (or English), Quality Medium, Reduce motion OFF, Master 90 %.
- Best take: 3 players on 3 PCs (van, tent, village), talking only through the in-game radio. Record the village PC, and record the van PC for the first shots.
- Fallback take (one PC): SOLO WITH BOTS. Village role for the main run (bots radio the order as text with dropped words), then Tent role on level 2 for the rumour shot.
- Clear `tutorial_done` only if you want the tips on screen (they look good in the tutorial shot).
- Do a dry run of level 1 first so you know where the villagers wait.

## Honest claims (use these, avoid stronger ones)

- Research reviews of disaster misinformation found that false messages often spread faster than official updates (JMIR Infodemiology 2025 systematic review), and that people struggle to tell disaster rumours from truth (University at Buffalo study). Sources are in `docs/research.md`.
- The game lets players **practise** read-back and source checking. It does not "fix" misinformation.

## Checklist after recording

- Clip is 2:00 or less; captions readable on a phone.
- No placeholder text on screen; credits screen visible for one second at the end if time allows.
- Export 1080p H.264, check audio levels (voice-over louder than rain).
